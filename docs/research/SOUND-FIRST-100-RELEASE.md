# Sound-first: 100 playable pairs

This delivery completes the first 100-pair preview collection: the six previously installed pairs plus 94 additions. The overall local catalogue now contains 435 unique fixed pairs (plus four external-channel seeds).

## Inventory

- 100 unique image/recording pairs; 99 distinct images; 33 distinct recordings.
- 30 recordings added in this release, approximately 211 minutes; 31 new recordings since the original 48-recording baseline, including the earlier duduk addition.
- Each recording appears in at most four pairs within this collection. Images are existing attributed assets, not newly acquired or generated pictures.
- Piano, harp, guitar, viola da gamba, guqin, duduk, accordion, restrained jazz, long-form ambient electronics and game-like synthesizer treatments. Historical compositions and contemporary productions are identified separately.
- Contemporary photography and open-film/game imagery sit alongside objects and paintings from different periods. Pairs are editorial comparisons, not claims of historical musical association.

## What remains limited

This is a playable preview, not a completed human listening panel. Full decoding and browser playback do not establish comfort through a whole working session. Loop seams, melody distraction, sudden entrances and fatigue remain to be auditioned. Notes flag some more active choices rather than calling every track equally unobtrusive.

26 of the 30 recordings added here are Kevin MacLeod productions. This expands timbres and durations substantially, but independent-artist and non-Western performer representation is still too concentrated. An instrument name, a title such as Dhaka, or a photograph's country does not prove the music originates in that culture. The other four additions are performances credited to Jujutacular, La Pianista, Membeth and Phillip W. Serna.

29 of the declared 40 research directions have at least an initial search attempt. Several leads were held for voice content, short duration, non-commercial restrictions, incomplete provenance or unsuitable intensity. This is discovery coverage, not 72.5% representation of human cultures. See the research ledger for individual results and gaps.

## Verification

- TypeScript check passed.
- 64 unit tests passed, including unique pairs, bounded reuse, source alignment, actual local files and SHA-256 verification of all 30 new recordings.
- 30 browser cases passed across the full run and targeted rerun. The 100-pair preview case checks every image decode, actual muted audio time advancement, original/translation reversibility, and timer-linked pause/resume.
- All 30 new MP3 files passed full strict FFmpeg decoding. One downloaded source MP3 (With the Sea) emitted a header warning during transcoding; its delivered output passes strict decoding. No compression, trimming or vocal removal was applied; constant attenuation is documented per file.
- Existing automatic rotation membership is preserved. The new tracks are curatedOnly and the preview collection is opt-in, so it does not reset the existing rotation bag or replace an active selection.

## Delivery and rollback

Install the packaged runtime only between sessions, on the existing fixed local address. Preserve the bound Codex chat and records. The installer keeps the previous version plus a SQLite backup, verifies live inventory and connection, and restores the previous launcher if readiness fails. The installed version and actual checks are recorded separately in the local delivery receipt; this document alone is not deployment evidence.

The UI remains original-first. Each pair includes sourced visual and recording facts, a separately folded editorial note, and an explicit Chinese translation toggle. No additional model call or new front-page explanation is introduced.

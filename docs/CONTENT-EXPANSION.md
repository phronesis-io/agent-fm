# Sound-first cultural expansion

Status: research and audit; no new selections released. Date: 2026-11-17.

## Product contract

A recording must be worth spending a working session with. Begin with sound, then choose a visual partner. Explore unfamiliar languages, cultural practices, periods and everyday settings without treating nationality as a musical style. Seek instrumental recordings of vocal traditions; do not assume unfamiliar lyrics are unobtrusive. No automatic vocal removal or invented cultural relationship.

The first delivery target is approximately 100 additional considered pairs, with genuinely new recordings rather than a Cartesian expansion of the existing library. This is a target, not permission to lower acceptance standards. Audit the existing 335 first. Larger milestones of 1,000 and 5,000 follow listening evidence.

## Scope and experience

Keep the current clock, controls, original-first descriptions and explicit translation. No additional homepage copy or automatic interpretation. Facts are reusable and sourced; editorial pairing notes remain separate. The user's Agent can explore these on invitation in the confirmed working language. Selection must not interrupt an active session. No desktop surveillance, new account system or central runtime service.

This phase changes content only. Downloadable packs, public submissions and host expansion are later engineering scopes, not implied implementations.

## Research coverage

Maintain a finite, versioned list of research directions with statuses: unsearched, searched, source-reviewed, audition-pending, rejected, accepted. Attempt at least 70% of that declared list before claiming broad exploration; search-query count does not measure coverage. Log a negative result as carefully as an accepted candidate. Do not extrapolate cultural coverage from English titles, tags or an instrument name.

Track original language, creative context, recording date and composition period separately. Explore contemporary and historical work, independent/game/animation scenes, urban and domestic settings, acoustic and electronic traditions. A gap stays a gap when sources or permission are missing.

## Acceptance

1. Identify exact composition, performance and recording; retain original titles and creator credit. Source links and reuse terms must correspond to that recording. A download link is not evidence of redistribution permission.
2. Assess full recording: voices, sudden entrances, dynamic changes, loop seams, fatigue, duration and work suitability. Technical audio metrics and muted playback are not listening evidence. Record who reviewed what and how much; unknown remains unknown.
3. Each pair names a specific audible/visual relationship, an alternative considered and an interruption risk. No generic prose templates, invented history or arbitrary national matching.
4. Validate media references, hashes, decoding, attribution, translations, duplicate pairs and catalogue identities. Preserve source text and locale fallback.
5. Preview candidates explicitly before adding to the default rotation. Human audition remains required for a final curated designation; technical tooling cannot confer it.

## Measurement

Report unique recordings, unique images and unique pairs separately, including reuse concentration. Track declared research directions attempted, evidence completeness, technical readiness and listening readiness separately. Existing content has unknown listening status unless an actual review record exists. Do not retrospectively label it rejected or approved.

Use skips, saves and revisits as ambiguous signals, never as proof of concentration or preference. Collect optional, concrete feedback after exposure. Evaluate disruptive moments, fatigue and repeat listening.

## Release and rollback

Research files are not runtime catalogue inputs. New media remains outside the default rotation until gates pass. A content release must list additions, source/credit records, checks, listening limits and version. Back up local data and retain the previous runtime; install between sessions, preserving the bound chat and fixed address. Roll back the release rather than rewriting personal history. Any changes to Agent recommendation/selection require a separate reviewed scope and the repository's delivery-impact evidence.

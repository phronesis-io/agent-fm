# Performer-led listening collection

2026-11-17. The previous 100-pair preview overused one composer. New timbres and titles are not evidence of cultural diversity.

Deliver a new local collection of 20 distinct recordings by at least 10 previously unrepresented credited creators/performers, with one intentional visual pairing per recording. Count artists by identity (aliases count once), not track count or instrument preset. Document the actual musical practice and source-backed location separately; neither a name nor a title proves ethnicity or tradition. Contemporary ambient, independent instrumental music and game-making communities belong alongside historical repertoire. This increment is not a claim to represent every culture.

Select unobtrusive instrumental material: no spoken introductions, anthems, battle music, dramatic percussion, isolated instrument demonstrations or short samples padded to meet duration. Require original source and redistribution permission, track-level credit, original title, English facts plus optional Chinese translation, and a distinct editorial note that remains folded. Do not claim human audition from technical playback checks. Keep an explicit listening-review limitation.

Deliver as an editorial collection searchable by title, performer and documented practice. Preserve original-first presentation and the current timer, session, queue and defaults. New sounds are curated-only; this does not change random rotation or Agent recommendation thresholds. Provide direct selection using the existing tested controls.

Verify source snapshots and license links, distinct media hashes, complete audio decoding, duration and loudness, all image references, program normalization, actual browser playback and pause/switch behavior. Test the packaged release and installed origin. Deploy between Pomodoros with a database backup and rollback to the previous runtime on readiness failure. Keep source metadata and rejection reasons in a reproducible research ledger. No external messages, account creation, payment or model calls are needed.
